How to use this kit

  1. Fill in the rules worksheet first, from your bylaws, declaration, and state law.
  2. Enter your meeting date and notice window. The checklist fills in planning targets for each stage.
  3. Fill in the notice and agenda sections. Anything left blank stays highlighted, so nothing slips through.
  4. Have your association's attorney review the notice before it goes out.
Before you startEvery legal requirement in this kit is a blank on purpose. Notice windows, delivery methods, quorum, proxies, and voting rules vary by governing documents and state law, and in some states a statute overrides the documents. Your documents, state law, and your association's attorney supply them.
